What the score checks — and what it doesn't

Checked daily

  • Canonical figures: VYRION Index 49.2 (v1.1 baseline, March 2026) and the CONSCIENCE Pledge Integrity Score 35.7 — never conflated
  • Data files parse and match what's displayed
  • Retired language stays retired outside historical record
  • Link health on cited sources
  • SHIELD feeds (USGS, NASA EONET) reachable
  • PWA health and social preview metadata

Not yet checked

  • Dark patterns / manipulative interface design
  • Undisclosed tracking
  • Accessibility regressions
  • Unlabeled simulated data in visual-only elements (maps, canvas)
  • Exact live-vs-local page parity

A score that hides its blind spots would overstate its own certainty. These are listed until they can be checked.
