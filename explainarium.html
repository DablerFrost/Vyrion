02 · THE INGREDIENTS OF THE SCORE

The eight Earth systems

Every VYRION Index number is a weighted blend of these eight. Together the weights sum to exactly 100%.

🧭

Two different eights — don't mix them up

VYRION has eight Earth systems (the ingredients that make up the Index score, on this page) and eight platform modules (the tools that actually go and do something about them, next section). They launched together and share a family resemblance, but they are not the same list.

ATLAS is the module that gathers live signals from all eight Earth systems below. SHIELD, ROOT, CANOPY, FLUX, TIDE, LOOP and PULSE are the other seven operating modules, each focused on a distinct planetary function. CONSCIENCE sits alongside the module stack as a separate accountability hub and supplies the Corporate Accountability system with a portfolio-mean signal. Nothing here is the same list twice.

08 · FROM DATA TO SIGNAL

How does VYRION actually work?

The simple version of a genuinely complicated pipeline.

01 / OBSERVE

Collect evidence

Satellites, sensors, public disclosures, and agencies like NOAA, NASA, USGS and GBIF supply raw measurements — daily, where a live feed exists.

02 / NORMALIZE

Make signals comparable

Different measurements speak different languages. Methodology converts each into a comparable 0–100 indicator score against the pre-industrial baseline.

03 / COMBINE

Build the Index from eight Earth systems

Six indicators build each Earth system. The eight weighted Earth systems build the Index. CONSCIENCE independently evaluates corporate commitments and feeds a portfolio-mean signal into the Corporate Accountability system.

04 / EXPLAIN

Publish the evidence — and the mistakes

The number is a doorway, not a destination. Every source is cited. Under the Open Proof Protocol, any error gets a public correction within 24 hours — nothing is ever deleted.
