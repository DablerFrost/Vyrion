VYRION Privacy Policy

Effective: October 25, 2026 · Operator: VYRION LLC (vyrion.earth) · Contact: contact@vyrion.earth

This policy describes VYRION as it operates today. VYRION is a public planetary-intelligence site: you can read it without an account, without logging in, and without giving us personal information.

The short version

  • Browsing vyrion.earth requires no personal information.
  • Your display preferences and your shop cart are stored on your own device, not on our servers.
  • We do not sell personal information. We do not run ads. We do not use data brokers.
  • If you buy something, your payment goes through Stripe and your order is printed and shipped through Printify — those providers handle the details they need to do their jobs, under their own policies.
  • Site-visit counting is done with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not track you across sites.

1. Browsing the site

You do not need an account to use vyrion.earth, and the site does not ask you for your name, email, or any other personal information to read its pages.

Our hosting providers (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) may log standard technical request data, such as IP addresses, for security and delivery of the site. VYRION does not use those logs to identify individual visitors.

2. Stored on your device, not with us

The following are stored in your own browser, on your device, and never sent to VYRION:

  • Display preferences: text size, light/dark theme, and Planetary Tone volume.
  • Whether you dismissed the install prompt.
  • Your shop cart contents.

If you install VYRION as an app, your device also caches site files so the app can open offline. Clearing the site's data in your browser, or uninstalling the app, removes all of the above.

3. Site-visit counting

VYRION's main pages load Cloudflare Web Analytics to count aggregate visits. It does not use cookies, does not build profiles of visitors, and does not track you across other sites. The shop does not load it.

4. Shop orders

When you buy from vyrion.earth/shop:

  • Payment is handled by Stripe Checkout. Your card details go directly to Stripe. VYRION never sees or stores your card number.
  • Printing and shipping are handled by Printify. Printify receives the order details it needs to produce and deliver your item, including your name and shipping address.
  • Order records (what was ordered, when, and for how much) are kept for fulfillment, customer support, and tax obligations.

Stripe and Printify each operate under their own privacy policies for the data they process.

5. Email

If you write to contact@vyrion.earth, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it. We do not add you to any mailing list unless you explicitly ask to be.

6. Live data on the pages

VYRION's pages display public data from sources such as NOAA, NSIDC, NASA, GBIF, Open-Meteo, Copernicus, FAO, and SEC EDGAR. When your browser loads live figures from those services, those services may see standard request data (such as your IP address). Their privacy policies apply to those requests.

7. What we never do

  • We never sell personal information.
  • We never run third-party advertising on vyrion.earth.
  • We never share personal information with data brokers.
  • We never require an account to read the site.

8. Your choices

  • Clear site data in your browser to remove stored preferences and cart contents.
  • Uninstall the app to remove the on-device cache.
  • Ask us at contact@vyrion.earth about an order or a message you sent us. We will answer, subject to any retention required for tax or legal reasons.

9. Children

VYRION is a general-audience site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has written to us, contact us and we will delete the message.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes will be noted at the top of this page.
